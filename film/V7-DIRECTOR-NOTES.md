- Thoughts must live in negative space ABOVE and AROUND the head.

Creative rule: the viewer should immediately read this as a man THINKING under pressure, not a dashboard.

Sound must explain visible action. No random sound bed.

Use restrained event-specific cues only:
- pressure: subtle low tension/heartbeat, not rattling;
- phone: realistic tactile contact/tap only when visible;
- CFO: subtle pen-on-paper texture when the writing action is visible;
- Operator: restrained decisive movement cue only if motivated by action;
- Future You/watch: clean mechanical watch tick synchronized to watch shot;
- Board reveal: one controlled restrained rise;
- brand ending: one tasteful resolved impact/tail.

Remove any sound that does not correspond to something the viewer can see or understand.

Music must be solemn, restrained, premium, and sparse. Avoid generic corporate inspiration, trailer booms, synthetic hype, or distracting AI-style crescendos. Music must duck beneath narration and genuine product audio.
